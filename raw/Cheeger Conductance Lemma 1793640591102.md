# Cheeger Conductance Lemma 1793640591102

The normalized graph Laplacian $L_{sym} = D^{-1/2} L D^{-1/2}$ reveals cluster structure via its second eigenvalue $\lambda_2$.

$$\frac{h(G)^2}{2} \le \lambda_2 \le 2h(G)$$

> [!THEOREM] Cheeger Inequality
> Bounds the conductance $h(G)$ using the spectral gap.

Connects directly to [[Graph Theory]] and [[Convex Optimization]].
