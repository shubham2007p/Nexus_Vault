# Large Language Model

AI models are a deep learning model in natural language processing. It makes the understanding of human language much easier and also gives specific attention to specific tasks in overall text.

It turns the input by encoder into the embeddings, and then those embeddings go to decoder and the response is made. LLM predict the next word and it learns the structure and the word around it from the large data it learned from.

LLMs are made by 2 things:
- Learning
- Fine-tuning

Fine learning is based on the old data, and then fine-tuning is based on the specific type of supervised data.

The large language model is turned overnight with the 2017 paper of the Google, “Attention is All You Need,” in which the transformer architecture is discussed.
